# Real-world 2026 data + long-term generation plan

## Goal
Seed a recognisable 2026 football world from open/public data while keeping the simulated future deterministic and legally traceable.

## Preferred public sources

### Current players and national teams
1. `torqueda/world_cup_player_tracker` — 2026 World Cup final-roster dataset, 48 national teams, confirmed/cut/replacement status, clubs, coaches, officials, provenance. Use as the strongest 2026 national-team snapshot where licensing/provenance permits redistribution.
2. `pochih/worldcup2026` — official-FIFA-derived 2026 roster snapshot with shirt number, broad/specific position, captain and FIFA player id. Use for cross-checking identities; do not mirror image URLs/assets into this repository.
3. `risingtransfers/world-cup-2026-data` — 48 national squads with club, age and performance-derived data. Use only fields whose licence/provenance permits redistribution; otherwise use as validation/reference.
4. `openfootball/players` — CC0/public-domain player identity dataset by country (name, position, DOB/height in many records). Preferred redistributable identity fallback.
5. `openfootball/clubs` — public-domain club/stadium identity normalization.

### Broader player universe
- `eatpizzanot/soccer-dataset` documents 182k+ players and 11k+ teams over 2012-2026. Treat as a discovery/identity/statistics source subject to its source/licence constraints; do not blindly vendor the full dataset.
- `withqwerty/open-football` is a catalogue of open football datasets and should be used to discover additional licensed sources.

### Youth / procedural generation references
- `Tahsan0619/nfm26`: 200+ procedural players per nation and nationality-aware name pools (16.6k+ first names / 37.1k+ surnames across 219 nationalities). Architectural reference for culturally plausible name generation and country-strength distributions; do not copy code/data without compatible licensing.
- `RankCA/FCManager`: combines real players with generated professionals for under-covered nations and nationality-aware youth academy generation. Architectural reference for coverage fallback and culturally adjacent name pools.
- `gaemi/agentic-fc`: deterministic seeded worlds, youth intake, careers and ordered RNG. Architectural reference for reproducible newgens.
- `hasandafa/football-data-lab`: synthetic players, youth academy and staff distributions. Reference for statistical distributions.

## Data policy
- Store provenance on every imported person: source repository, source id, snapshot date, licence class and confidence.
- Prefer CC0/public-domain identity data for data vendored into this repo.
- Public facts can be normalized from multiple sources, but do not copy copyrighted descriptions, proprietary ratings, photos, badges or protected database dumps.
- Real-world snapshot ends at the configured 2026 cut-off. After that, transfers, development, retirements, newgens, staff careers and national-team choices are generated by the simulation.
- Real names must never be used for procedurally generated future people.

## Import architecture
`RawSourceAdapter -> IdentityNormalizer -> EntityResolver -> ProvenanceLedger -> CanonicalPlayer/Staff -> Club/NationalTeam membership`

Identity resolution priority:
1. stable public source id when reusable;
2. normalized full name + DOB + nationality;
3. normalized full name + club + position + age;
4. unresolved records remain separate and enter a review queue rather than being merged aggressively.

## Newgen V3 requirements
The existing V2 generator is Brazil-only for names and should be replaced/augmented by V3:
- nationality/culture-specific name pools;
- birth city/region weighted by population and club recruitment reach;
- age 14-19 academy pipeline rather than only instant senior-ready intake;
- height/body development distributions by position and age;
- preferred foot and weak-foot probability;
- secondary positions derived from archetype;
- technical/mental/physical/GK correlated attribute families;
- hidden personality: professionalism, ambition, pressure, consistency, injury susceptibility, adaptability, loyalty, controversy;
- socioeconomic/family context hooks without deterministic stereotypes;
- academy quality affects floor, coaching affects development rate, recruitment affects search space rather than simply boosting PA;
- rare talent sampled from a heavy-tailed distribution; elite academies increase discovery probability but do not manufacture guaranteed superstars;
- country football strength and population influence pool size, not individual destiny;
- late bloomers and early maturers;
- deterministic seed per person;
- yearly retirement/death-of-career replacement target so world population remains stable.

## Staff generation requirements
Generate coaches, assistants, GK coaches, fitness coaches, analysts, scouts, physios, doctors, psychologists, nutritionists, youth coaches, loan managers, recruitment directors, technical directors, football coordinators and performance staff.

Sources of future staff:
- retiring players (career/position/personality influence their staff profile);
- academy/coaching graduates with no playing career;
- former referees for selected analytical/administrative roles;
- existing staff career progression.

Staff attributes should be correlated with education/licences, experience, playing background, languages, personality and role; reputation must be dynamic rather than generated as a fixed permanent tier.

## Youth data strategy
Real youth players may be imported only when an open, sufficiently reliable and licensable source exists. Do not fabricate real minors. Missing academy squads are represented by generated fictional prospects. At the 2026 cut-off, known public senior/registered youth players can coexist with fictional academy depth, clearly tagged by provenance.
